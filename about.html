About Ebony Rose

A friendly salon in Monrovia where good hair, nails and makeup are done with care.

Our story

Ebony Rose started with a simple idea: everyone should leave the salon feeling confident and cared for. We focus on healthy hair, neat work and a relaxed atmosphere, whether you come for braids, a silk press, nails or full bridal glam.

What we stand for

1

Healthy hair first

No style is worth damaging your hair. We say so if a look is too tight.

2

Clean work

Neat parts, tidy edges and finishing you can be proud of.

3

Respect for your time

Book a slot and we keep to it.

What a visit looks like

1. Welcome and consultation

We talk about the style, the length, the time it will take and the price before we start.

2. Your service

Sit back and relax. You can charge your phone, chat or rest while we work.

3. Finishing and aftercare

We finish the style and give you simple tips to keep it looking fresh for longer.
