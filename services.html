Good to know

How should I prepare for braids?

Come with clean, detangled, blow-dried hair if you can. If not, tell us and we can wash and dry it first for a small extra fee.

Do I need to bring my own hair extensions?

You can bring your own or ask us what to buy. Message us on WhatsApp before your visit and we will tell you the type and amount you need.

How long will my style last?

Most braids last 4 to 8 weeks with good care. Silk press lasts 1 to 2 weeks. Gel nails last about 2 to 3 weeks.

Can I change or cancel my appointment?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at least a day before and we will move you to a new time.

How do I pay?

Tell us which payment options you want to use when you book. We confirm everything on WhatsApp.
